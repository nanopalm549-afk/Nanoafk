The Method

Every session follows the same dialectic

i.

You bring a question

Type it, speak it, or photograph the page it came from.

ii.

Socratic asks one back

Instead of the answer, you get the next question — the one that narrows the gap in your own reasoning.

iii.

The idea gets a shape

A generated diagram, a spoken explanation, or an illustrated study sheet — whichever form fits what you're stuck on.

iv.

You're tested on it, honestly

The exam room checks whether the understanding actually held, not just whether the conversation felt good.
